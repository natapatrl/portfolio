Hello, I'm Natapatr Lorsathian (Nat for short).

I'm a versatile game designer. While proficient with the basics of every design discipline, I enjoy UI/UX and level design the most.

Ask me anything below!

What makes a game great to you?

I firmly believe that a great game must first and foremost feel good to play.

While fun is undoubtedly the foundation of game design, even the most enjoyable game can quickly become frustrating if it's not user-friendly.

So what's your design goal?

Striking a balance between fun and user experience is key to creating a truly great gameplay experience.

What tools do you work with?

Unity, Unreal (including UEFN, Fortnite's level editor) and Figma, which I use to prototype UI.

What are you working on right now?

Anonymized! It started as our Global Game Jam 2026 entry and won 1st place at Game Dev Hub Bangkok.

Now it's being developed into a full game, and the demo is already on Steam.
