Peritoneum

Entry: [IndieCritical] Summer Jam 2025 / 2025 / Team Project

1.0Overview

Peritoneum is an experimental platformer created under the jam's theme, "Not What It Seems." It subverts the traditional platformer tropes by introducing unexpected mechanics.

Rather than aiming for a convention and polished game, my jam team (formed mostly from strangers) focused on experimentation and fun. As such, the game's design reflects this philosophy; the core mechanic uses a gun's blast to propel the character towards a direction.

Designing around that logic, action elements are blended in to create a more satisfying and complete experience of using a firearm. A controllable directional propulsion pack was also added to give players greater movement feel and control.

2.0Work History

  • Curated 5 progressively challenging levels
  • Implemented a diegetic tutorial that encourages the player to experiment and test things out on their own
  • Designed a boss fight encounter using state machines, working closely with the programmer for its implementation
  • Worked on the various game mechanics, such as enemy attacks and player abilities
  • Coordinated workflow between both programmers and artists as the creative lead
