Unpuzzled Business

Escape Room Puzzle Game / 2024 / Team Project

1.0Overview

Unpuzzled Business is a supernatural-themed escape room puzzle game developed by a team of nine.

I served as both gameplay and level designer, while also leading the project as creative director.

My primary challenge was transforming a vague concept left to the team by a former member into a cohesive, playable experience.

3.0Puzzle Flowchart

The first task I completed for the team was creating the puzzle flowchart, which outlined each puzzle step alongside detailing its theme, mechanics, related items, and event triggers.

This flowchart significantly improved the team's workflow, enabling all roles to work in parallel and eliminating many of the bottlenecks that had previously slowed development.

4.0Player Guidance

After the completion of the first playable prototype, a recurring issue emerged around the clarity of the puzzles and mechanics.

I have identified that this is largely due to the game's immersive and diegetic design hindering the communication of various game mechanics.

To address this, I conducted user research and playtesting focused on player guidance. Based on the feedback and analysis, I improved the level's readability by adjusting lighting, incorporating leading lines, and simplifying certain puzzle elements to enhance clarity without compromising immersion.

5.0Lighting Problems

With all the lighting improvements and changes comes more issues. One such issue concerns the game's performance due to the heavy use of volumetric and moveable lighting, in order to improve player guidance.

After discussing the issue with programmers, I have created a lighting guideline that details the limitations of light placement in each room. I also have a hand in editing and optimizing the lighting in-engine, utilizing my knowledge on lighting in Unreal Engine.
